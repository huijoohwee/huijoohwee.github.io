# Lean business guideline applicability

## PRD

Make the existing business guidance usable one decision at a time. Preserve universality, functional
neutrality, domain and provider agnosticity, modular ownership, evidence and effect authority. A phase
or first payment must not require unrelated projections, architecture or a full business plan.

The accepted outcome is source guidance with decision-triggered loading and coherent successor rules;
it makes no demand, financial viability, runtime or deployment claim.

reconcile to the model. Restrict the runtime-breach condition to autonomous incident diagnosis.
